## 🇬🇧 English

The knowledge base under the film-grammar toolchain: nine chapters and about 380 professional terms covering narrative technique, composition and the 180° axis, lighting systems, a shot dictionary, narrative perspective, mise-en-scène, editing rhythm, acting systems, and directing craft — plus an AI correction list and a production checklist. Chapters are split into independent files loaded on demand, capped at three at a time so context does not blow up. It runs in four modes: term lookup, prompt-building support, AI video quality triage (unnatural acting, flat expressions, mechanical camera moves), and pre-production style planning. It also ships a visual-style quick table and a medium lock that hands you ready English keywords plus the NOT exclusions you must write alongside them; it supplies knowledge only and never generates images, video, or audio itself.

### ✨ What it does

- Nine chapters and about 380 terms spanning narrative, composition and axis, lighting, shot dictionary, perspective, mise-en-scène, editing rhythm, acting, and directing
- Chapters split into independent on-demand files, max three per load, with a user-scenario-to-chapter mapping table
- Four modes: term lookup, prompt-building support, AI video quality triage, and pre-production style planning
- Appendix A holds 18 fixes across 6 categories of common AI video faults, each with cause, prompt fix, and expected result
- Appendix B provides a 26-item checkable production checklist across 4 categories for pre-generation review
- Visual-style quick table (cyberpunk, Hong Kong night rain, IMAX epic, symmetric pastel, Nordic minimal, 70s film, film noir, naturalistic) plus a medium lock for photoreal, 2D anime, and 3D render
